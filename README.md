# 👻 A2ThreeD – STL Files

Welcome to the official STL repository for **A2ThreeD** 3D Design files.

This repo contains a selection of 3D-printable files for fans, builders, and prop enthusiasts looking to create high-quality Ghostbusters gear, and other projects.

## 🧰 What’s Included

You’ll find a mix of:

- Proton Pack components
- Wand / Neutrona accessories
- Mounts, adapters, and brackets
- Replacement or upgrade parts
- Experimental / prototype designs

> Some designs may be simplified or alternate versions of parts used in full builds.

## 🖨️ Printing Recommendations

These files are primarily designed for FDM printers.

**Baseline settings:**
- Layer height: `0.2mm`
- Walls: `3–4 perimeters`
- Infill: `15–30%` (increase for structural parts)
- Material: PLA, PETG, or ABS depending on use
- Supports: As required (check orientation)

> ⚠️ For prop parts that take stress (mounts, clips, etc.), PETG or ABS is recommended.


## 🔧 Assembly Notes

- Some parts may require sanding, trimming, or post-processing
- Test fit before final assembly
- Hardware (screws, inserts, magnets, etc.) may be required but not always included
